Scratch & Smile is a casual scratch card game that uses virtual coins only.

- No real-money gambling: coins cannot be purchased, sold or exchanged for money, goods or prizes. There are no in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts and no login.
- Simulated gambling is declared in the age rating questionnaire.
- All data (coin balance, statistics, settings) is stored on the device only; the app collects no user data.

How to test: on first launch the app opens a complimentary Bronze ticket. Scratch the foil with your finger; the ticket is revealed once about 75% of the surface is scratched. Back on the main screen you can claim the daily bonus, open any ticket and tap "Buy ticket". In Settings, "Tap to reveal" replaces scratching with a single tap (accessibility option).

Share bonus (optional): the "Share" tile opens the system share sheet with a text and the App Store link. When the user completes a share in one of the listed social apps, the app adds virtual coins once per network. Sharing is never required to use the app.

The app is localized in English, Romanian, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese (Brazil) and follows the device language.
